# Ten Cells 0.19.0: 25 energy makers, Buy max, slower start

## Energy makers up to tier 25
- Eleven new makers after Genesis Forge: World Smithy, Comet Foundry, Spiral Dynamo, Horizon Siphon, Prism Vault, Aether Orb, Halo Array, Shard Cathedral, Lens of Ages, Glitch Engine and Crown of Cells.
- Each makes 1,000× the one before. The last three cost more than 1e308, so they need Break Infinity.
- **Tier 11 and up now unlock like tiers 1-10**: own one of the tier before. Before, tier 11 waited for the Fractal dimension, 9th of 12, which is why it never showed up.
- Each new maker gets five tree upgrades and five achievements, like the others.
- Fabricator V (tiers 15-19) and Fabricator VI (tiers 20-25) autobuy them.

## Quality of life
- **Buy max** in every prestige shop: Fragment perks, Singularity, Ascension, Void, the Infinity tree and Ultra. It buys the cheapest thing you can afford until nothing's left. It never buys the Omega Engine, so you can't end the game by accident.
- The "Unknown signal" card says when you'll afford the maker that reveals the next tier.
- A note pops up when a new energy maker appears (one note even if several appear at once).

## Slower start
The first Rebirth came in about 11 minutes and runs got strong very fast.
- First Rebirth needs **1e12** energy (was 1e10). Lowered Threshold brings it to 1e9 (was 1e7).
- Makers 1-10 make **40%** of what they did.
- Head Start gives 100×5^level starting energy (was 100×10^level, which at max level skipped the whole first goal).
- Simulated active player (4 taps/s, buying everything): first Rebirth at about 25 minutes (was 11), the next few every 10-15 minutes (was 5-8), Singularity at about 8 hours (was about 4).
- If you're mid-game, your current run will feel slower for a bit, since makers 1-10 make less.

## Lighter
- The 3D pet and crew renderer asks for the low-power GPU and no longer keeps a spare copy of every frame. Same picture.
