# Ten Cells 0.17.0: story and characters for the new worlds

Live on the Claude link and at https://bitflux28.github.io/ten-cells/

## New chapters (27–30 of 42)
They sit right after "After the Wall", the first chapter past Infinity, and before "The Aeon Wyrm". Each one has an opening, a middle scene and an ending.

| # | Chapter | Lead | What happens | Missions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 27 | Smaller Copies | Facet | The record should end at the multiverse, but the Engine keeps pulling. A crystal that repeats itself smaller and smaller says hello (hello). | Unlock Fractal, grow Shards to 1e12 |
| 28 | Through the Glass | Alev | A silver lake reflects Vela, and the reflection talks back. She never stops tapping. | Unlock Mirror, grow Reflections to 1e12 |
| 29 | Lullaby Engine | Morrow | A sleepy giant has been dreaming whole universes. Nobody wake him up. | Unlock Dream, grow Reverie to 1e12, come back after 12+ hours away |
| 30 | First Light | Lumen | At the bottom of everything is the light that lit every engine. Nyx is scared of it. | Unlock Origin, grow Genesis to 1e12 |

Old characters show up too: Archivist, Kiro, Juno, Bram, Echo, Nyx and Sable.

## New characters (3D, in Crew › Cast)
- **Facet**: a floating crystal with smaller copies of itself circling, and smaller ones circling those
- **Alev**: Vela's reflection, mirrored, in teal with silver hair
- **Morrow**: round, sleepy, in a nightcap, holding a lantern, with little z's floating up
- **Lumen**: a glowing core inside turning rings and rays; it pulses harder when Origin flares

## More energy from the new worlds
Each new world now multiplies all production by (1 + OoM of its resource) to a power:
- Fractal ^2 (×961 at 1e30 Shards)
- Mirror ^2.5 (×5,351)
- Dream ^3 (×29,791)
- Origin ^4 (×923,521; was ^2)

All four together at 1e30 come to about ×10^17. Their old side effects (cores, Void Essence, offline cap) still apply.
