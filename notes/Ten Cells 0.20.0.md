# Ten Cells 0.20.0

## Daily rewards
- A 7-day cycle, one claim per calendar day. Missing a day never resets anything: the next reward just waits.
- Each reward has its own 3D model: Spark Cell (10 min of energy), Battery (30 min), Pet Egg, Fragment Crystal, **Double Day** (all energy ×2 for 24 hours), Hourglass (2 hours of energy) and Golden Crown (×3 for 6 hours plus a rare egg).
- A card on the Engine screen shows today's reward. Tap it for the full calendar.

## Permanent upgrades
- 12 upgrades bought with energy that no prestige ever resets, not even Ultra.
- Production ×1.5 up to ×100, tap power, cheaper makers, longer offline time, and more Fragments, Singularities and Infinity Points.
- A gold "Permanent" chip at the front of the Upgrades tab, a gold bar on the Engine screen with the next one, and their own line in Stats.

## Automation (AFK)
- Each auto-prestige fires on **Gain** (at least X), on a **Timer** (every N minutes of the run), or at **× have** (gain at least N× what you hold).
- New Fragment perk **Rebirth Autopilot** (60 Fragments) unlocks Auto-Rebirth early.
- Its own **Auto** tab on PC, with a live "fires in…" line under each rule.

## On PC
- Bosses, Pets and Auto get their own tabs.
- The game column is 700 px (was 560). Still centred, not stretched.
- Windows app: **Collapse** (header button or C) hides the game. A small animated atom sits in the bottom-right corner of the screen with your energy. The game keeps earning without drawing anything. Click the atom to come back, drag it to move it, or hover and press × to quit.
- Window size presets in Settings work in the Windows app.

## Settings > Graphics
- 30 or 60 fps. 30 draws half as often; animations keep their speed.
- Pixel-size slider (2 to 6) with a live preview box showing the sky behind it at that pixel size.

## Also
- Busier sky in the same style: far galaxies, star clusters, a ringed planet, drifting asteroids, a satellite and the odd comet, coloured to each world.
- 30 very hard achievements, ending with "Ten Cells, Complete" (unlock every other one).
- Story: the later "spend time with the Engine" missions ask for much less (chapter 10: 3.5 hours to 2, chapter 24: 14 hours to 4.3). The "play on different days" missions are gone.
- The upgrade tree view goes back to the middle after a prestige.
- Upgrade tree overlap fix (0.19.1).
